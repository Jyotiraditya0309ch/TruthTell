Now , understanding the working behind the scenes
 ### For authenticating text based news we are using Multimodal rather than relying one.It compromises of:
1) News Checker:scrape  real time news articles using posted article.Compare the scraped news article and posted article using LLM to deciede whether same msg is being conveyed or not.
2) Structure analyzer: Extract features such as grammatical errors,punctuation errors,no. Of hastags,no of urls and provide it to gradient boosting model to get result.
3) Semantic Aanalyzer: Tokenize text,embed it through glove embeding,pass it through multi lstm to get results.
4) Evidence verifier: Scraped the supported evidence if provided and compare it with published news artciles using LLM to get results.

Here Semantic and structured analyzers are built from scratch,  others use built in llm features.
